2. Opt-In and Message Frequency

End-users opt in to receive SMS responses by texting START or sending an initial command directly to the service number. Message frequency varies depending on user-initiated workflow executions.

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan. Check with your wireless provider for details regarding your text messaging plan.

4. Opt-Out and Help Instructions

You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP to any message. After sending STOP, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. For support, reply HELP directly to any message.
